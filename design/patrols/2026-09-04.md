## What was clean

- Every colour, radius, shadow and type size in the two new stylesheets comes
  from a token. No hardcoded colour anywhere.
- The three off-scale pixel values in the diff are all named as such in a
  comment beside them, and all three are geometries this registry itself fixes:
  the rail's 232px, and the sound switch's 36 by 20 track with its 16 knob.
- No component was rebuilt instead of reused. The `Setting error line` is one
  shape used in three places, the `Keycap` and `Mono badge` primitives are the
  registered ones, and the focus ring is the shared one.
- `python design/check-contrast.py`: all 59 pairs pass. Everything registered
  today reuses a pair the audit already covered, and each row names which one,
  with **one exception found while the row was being built**: nothing had ever
  measured the light `Primary button` primitive. The pill, sign in and first run
  all use its on-dark twin, so its label, its nothing-to-do-yet fill and the dim
  end of its busy pulse had gone unaudited since the day it was registered.
  Those three and the paste field's accent edge and accent sentence are now
  five new pairs, and all five pass: 18.11:1, 5.13:1, 9.66:1, 6.72:1, 6.72:1.
  This is the classic shape of a gap in the system rather than carelessness, and
  it was only visible because a light surface finally needed a primary button.
