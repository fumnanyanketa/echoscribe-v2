# Drift patrol, 2026-10-01: the comp returns as the authority

The user re-shared the original Instrument comp (the Claude Design export,
"EchoScribe v2 — direction: Instrument") and instructed that the built app
match it **pixel for pixel**. Four decisions were put to them and answered:

1. **The comp's exact values win over the rounded token scale.** The scale
   in `src/styles.css` now carries the comp's own steps (12.5, 13.5, 14.5)
   plus `--text-nano` (10.5) and `--text-meta` (11.5), and the radii gained

   the comp draws a value no token holds, the component states the literal
   with a comment naming the comp element. design-system.md's mandate rule 1
   is amended accordingly.
2. **The comp's violet statics win over the strict violet rule.** The brand
   dot, the active nav item's lead bar, the account initials disc, the
   signed-in dot, the key field's edge and dot, and the step bars are now
   violet, as drawn. design-system.md's violet rule carries the dated
   exception. Two resulting pairs fail 4.5:1 and are recorded as deliberate
   in `check-contrast.py`, with the comp's own redundancy reasoning.
3. **Comp content with nothing behind it is planned, not faked**: plan rows
   6 to 11 (receiving app, filters, device row, rebind and hold-to-talk,
   scratchpad, error pill actions), each needing `/architect` first.
4. **Recorded placements stay**: sign out stays in the rail foot and the
   Deepgram key stays on the Transcription surface; only their styling
   moved to the comp's language.
