# Patrol note, 2026-10-02: the app icon

EchoScribe shipped the Tauri scaffold icon until tonight. Three candidates
were drawn from the locked tokens (the dark glass tile #16171c with the
#34363f edge, the violet channel #7c5cff): the level meter, the pill in
miniature, and the breathing signal. The user chose the level meter.

The 1024px master is `design/icon-1024.png`. The platform set in
`src-tauri/icons/` is generated from it with `npx tauri icon
design/icon-1024.png`; regenerate from the master, never edit the set by
hand. The iOS and Android sets the generator also emits are deleted on
purpose: mobile is out of scope (AGENTS.md).

Registered: `App icon` — the level meter motif on the dark glass tile,
violet bars at the meter's own radius, no wordmark, no letter. It is the
one asset that may show the violet channel while no microphone is open,
because an icon is a name, not a state.
