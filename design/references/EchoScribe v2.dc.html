EchoScribe v2 — direction: Instrument

A two-tone shell — graphite where you navigate, white where you read — and a dark-glass pill. Manrope + JetBrains Mono, one violet channel. The pill is a measuring instrument, not a chat window: a grip rail you can grab from any edge, a live level meter that only ever means “sound is arriving”, and one line of text where the locked front of the sentence is ink and the still-settling tail is grey with a dotted rule under it. The pill is deliberately the darkest thing on your screen — against a white document it reads as a physical object laid on the page rather than a panel grafted into it, and it needs no border tricks to stay legible. Violet is reserved exclusively for “the mic is open”, so it can never be confused with decoration. Contrast table at the bottom.

Smallest form, 232×44. Off state is this pill absent from the screen entirely — nothing grey-and-idle to mistake for it. The breathing violet ring plus the literal words MIC OPEN are two independent signals for the same fact.
2 · Listening
ship the migration on  thursday morn
Final text is ink, flush left and stable. Interim text is grey with a dotted underline — the underline is the promise that it can still change; it disappears the instant Deepgram finalises, so the line visibly “hardens” left to right. The meter is amplitude only, never a spectrogram: 18 bars, 60 ms updates, flat at silence.
3 · Long dictation
, and then hand the release notes to Priya before the standup so she can  post them in the
0:41 · 84 words
Decision: truncate from the left with a soft fade, never scroll and never grow. The tail is the only part still in question, so the tail keeps the right edge; history is already recorded elsewhere and the real text is in your document. A small elapsed/word counter appears after 20 s so long dictations feel accounted for.
4 · Errors
NO KEY Deepgram key missing.
NO MIC No microphone detected.
DROPPED Connection lost. Kept “…on thursday”
Error pills keep the exact geometry of the working pill and swap the violet channel for one coloured edge, a mono code, one sentence of plain cause, and exactly one action. The dropped-connection case states what it managed to keep, because silent loss is the worst failure a dictation tool can have.
Surface 2 — dashboard, 1200 × 800
Navigation: a persistent left rail, not tabs. History is a working surface you return to with a query in your head, and Settings is the section that will keep growing (deeper customisation, per-language rules, vocabulary). A rail keeps the search field anchored in one place and absorbs new sections without re-laying-out a tab bar. Account is pinned to the bottom because it is visited twice a year.

Moving the pill to dark glass fixed the tightest pair in the old palette: interim text is now #a1a5b4 at 7.6:1 (was 5.1:1), so the grey carrying the “this can still change” meaning no longer trades legibility for softness. Two failures remain, both flagged above and both deliberate.
