EchoScribe

Press a hotkey anywhere on Windows, speak, and the words appear where your cursor already was. In your email, your document, your chat. No switching windows, no copying and pasting.

Your own Deepgram account

EchoScribe sends your voice to Deepgram for transcription using a key from your own free Deepgram account. There is no EchoScribe server in between.

Your words stay on your machine

Your history, your custom words and your settings live in one file on your computer. Audio is never saved, by EchoScribe or anywhere it controls.

Sign in with email or Google

An account keeps your words and settings together across restarts. Sign-in uses a one-time email code or your Google account, and EchoScribe never sees a password.
