ONE THING WORTH KNOWING ABOUT BRINGING A WINDOW FORWARD

On an earlier attempt the small window rose above the dashboard but did not take
focus and did not reach the front of the desktop. At that moment the foreground
window belonged to another process's dialog, and Windows refuses a foreground
change to a process that does not hold the foreground lock. With an ordinary
window in front instead, as above, it came fully forward every time. So the
behaviour is right, and it is at the mercy of a Windows rule nothing in this app
controls.
