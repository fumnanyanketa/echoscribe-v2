AC-4, the app shell, 2026-09-03
Resize it, drag it somewhere, close the app, reopen it, and it comes back the
same size in the same place on the same screen.

VERDICT: blocked, and the single screen half is met and is written out below.
This machine has one monitor, so "on the same screen" cannot be told apart from
"on the only screen", and the record's own milestone 2 names two monitors of
different sizes as this criterion's proof. Nothing here was worked around.

Physical pixels throughout. 200% display scaling, working area 3840x2064.


THE JOURNEY, AS A PERSON WOULD DO IT

1. The window was dragged by its title bar. Real mouse input through the
   ordinary Windows path, so Windows moved the window and the app read the
   result back.

     $ drag.ps1 -FromX 714 -FromY 41 -ToX 1614 -ToY 441
     before: outer 114,12 1946x1351
     after : outer 1014,412 1946x1351      moved by exactly +900,+400

   The row the app wrote, 400 ms after the drag stopped:

     (..., 960, 640, 0.5174479166666667, 0.5268895348837209, '\\.\DISPLAY1',
      '2026-09-03T09:20:03Z')

   Checked rather than trusted: the window's centre is
   (1014 + 973, 412 + 675.5) = (1987, 1087.5); 1987/3840 = 0.5174479 and
   1087.5/2064 = 0.5268895. The stored fractions are the observed centre.

2. The window was resized by a real drag on its bottom right corner.

     $ drag.ps1 -FromX 2959 -FromY 1762 -ToX 3300 -ToY 1900
     after : outer 1014,412 2287x1489
             client 2261x1418               (1130.5x709 logical)

     row: (..., 1131, 709, 0.5618489583333334, 0.5603197674418605,
           '\\.\DISPLAY1', '2026-09-03T09:20:15Z')

3. The app was closed with the window's own close button, which is AC-4's own
   wording, "close the app".

     $ click.ps1 -X 3241 -Y 441        the X in the title bar
     --- process after clicking close ---
     NO ECHOSCRIBE PROCESS (the app exited)

     row rewritten at close: (..., 1131, 709, 0.5618489583333334,
       0.5603197674418605, '\\.\DISPLAY1', '2026-09-03T09:20:39Z')

   So closing the dashboard closes the app, and the geometry is written before
   the exit rather than lost by it. Both are the second amendment's decisions.

4. The app was started again.

     === after reopen ===
     outer      : 1014,412 2288x1489
     client     : 2262x1418
     row unchanged, still stamped 09:20:39

     position   : 1014,412 closed  ->  1014,412 reopened     identical
     client size: 2261x1418 closed ->  2262x1418 reopened    1 px wider

The one pixel is the rounding of a half logical pixel. The window was left at
1130.5 logical pixels wide, the column is an integer, so 1131 was stored and
1131 came back. It is half a logical pixel and cannot be seen. It is written
down here so that nobody reading "identical" later finds the discrepancy and
wonders.

WHAT IS NOT PROVEN HERE
That the window returns to the screen it was left on, when there is more than
one screen to be left on. That needs the two monitor sitting the record asks
for, and it is the half the record itself warns will be tempting to skip.
