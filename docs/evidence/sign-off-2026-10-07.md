# Sign-off: Sign in and Dictate with a hotkey, 2026-10-07

**Who this is for.** The person who decides whether EchoScribe goes to anyone
besides themselves. The project's rules put these two features in the highest
risk tier, because one holds your account and the other sends your voice to an
outside service. For that tier an AI may gather the proof but may not approve
it. You do, at the bottom of this page.

**What your signature means.** Not "everything is perfect". It means: I have
read what was proved and what was not, and I accept giving EchoScribe to a
small circle of people, on their own computers, with the gaps below known.
You can also sign "not yet", which is just as useful.

**How the proof was gathered.** Every "proved" below was seen happening in
the real app, not reasoned from the code. Each one names the date and the
report where the proof lives. "Not proved" means nobody has watched it happen
yet. It does not mean it is broken.

## Part 2. Dictate with a hotkey (record 0002, 33 promises)

**Proved: 25. Proved in part: 4. Not proved yet: 4.**

The full detail is in `dictate-with-a-hotkey/report.md` (2026-09-02, you were
at the keyboard for every voice and sound moment), `end-to-end-2026-10-01/report.md`,
and today's frames.

### Proved

- **The core promise.** Double tap Ctrl, the pill appears within half a
  second with a moving waveform, you hear the start sound, you speak, and
  the words type themselves where your cursor was, in another app. Proved
  many times, in English and in German, and in your own production
  dictation on 2026-10-05.
- **It never types into a password box.** Spoken into a real password
  field: nothing typed, the pill said why, nothing saved to history.
- **It never takes back what it typed**, and unfinished words stay grey on
  the pill and are never typed.
- **Your cursor stays yours.** Clicking or dragging the pill never moves
  focus away from the app you are typing in.
- **It stops on its own** after 30 seconds of silence or 5 minutes in all.
- **When something goes wrong it says so plainly**: microphone blocked or
  missing, Deepgram unreachable, key refused, connection dropped mid
  sentence. Each one was forced on purpose and seen. Words already typed
  were never lost.
- **Your key.** Checked with Deepgram before saving, refused if wrong, and
  afterwards only its last four characters are ever shown (seen 2026-10-06:
  "caf3").
- **Your history** is saved and survives restarts (2026-10-01).
- **Settings** offer exactly two hotkeys, Ctrl or Alt, either side of the
  keyboard, and one switch for the sounds (seen 2026-10-06).
- **Signed out, the hotkey does nothing.**

### Proved in part

- **The pill remembers where you drag it.** Proved on one screen. Carrying
  that across two monitors needs a second monitor.
- **Errors offer the right button.** The buttons are right. The microphone
  privacy page actually opening was not watched.
- **A second account sees none of your dictations.** Checked in the
  database on 2026-10-06: the new account owns none of the 39. Its history
  screen was not looked at, because it has no key yet.
- **A key that stops working mid dictation.** A real Deepgram refusal was
  seen and handled. A spent free allowance cannot be produced on purpose
  without using up a real account.

### Not proved yet

- **Anything involving two monitors** (three promises): the pill first
  appearing on the screen you are working on, staying put when you move
  between screens, and never ending up off screen when one is unplugged.
  Needs a second monitor.
- **Switching Windows' default microphone mid use.** Skipped at your choice
  on 2026-08-30.
- **Also never tested, though not a written promise:** slow internet
  during a dictation. Only a full drop was tested.
