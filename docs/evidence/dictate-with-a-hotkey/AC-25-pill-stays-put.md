# AC-25: once open, the pill stays where it is for the whole dictation

Live run, 2026-08-29.

## Outcome: met

## What was observed

Step 7 part 1. The pill was open on the larger monitor. Without touching
the pill, the user clicked into a window on the other monitor.

User: "The pill stayed in its position."

It did not move and it did not vanish when focus went to the other screen.

## Scope of this evidence

Milestone 1 has no microphone, so "for the whole dictation" here means
"for the whole time the pill is open". The longer running case, with audio
and transcription going, cannot be tested until those milestones exist.
